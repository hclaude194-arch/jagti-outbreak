# Jagti Outbreak: notes for Claude

Unity 6 (6000.0 LTS) URP project. The owner tests it in Unity on a laptop with Intel Iris Xe graphics, 16 GB RAM. Unity is not available in the cloud container, so nothing can be run there (a type check is possible, see below). Keep C# conservative, and say plainly in PRs that code is untested until the owner presses Play.

- Layout source of truth: `tools/layout/generate_layout.py` → `Assets/Data/campus_layout.json`. Map units are about 1 m. The origin is the central roundabout (map 414, 996), and map up is +Z. After editing, bump `VERSION` (Unity rebuilds the scene when it rises), then run `generate_layout.py` and `generate_terrain.py` in that order. Roads go through `build_roads()` (2026-11-20, owner said roads were broken at the roundabout and junctions): each of `ROUNDABOUTS` gets a closed ring road (`Roundabout Ring - <name>`) round a kerbed island, and roads meet it at `ring_at()`; a road end that joins another is carried to its centre line and gets flared corners (per-point `left`/`right` half widths in the JSON, left out on the sharp side of a fork); at an L the wider road runs on past the corner; each road gets a `layer` above the roads that join it (Unity lifts by layer). Every road consumer reads the flares: generate_terrain (`road_sides`, junction height blend, `ROAD_LEVELS`), `CampusGreyboxBuilder.Line`/`InsideOtherRoad`, build_outdoor `edge_dist`, render_map_tour `strip`/`kerbs`. The girls' hostel lane forks off the spine road south of the courts and runs at the hostels' level (`ROAD_LEVELS`) to Girls Hostel A's south door (owner's photos 5 and 6); beside the spine (within `ROAD_LEVEL_FOLLOW`) it keeps the spine's height so neither edge drops into a bank. Roundabout islands are low kerbed grass mounds (`MakeRoundaboutIsland`), never slabs on the road; the gate's island is the gate model's flower bed. The central roundabout has exactly four arms (owner, 2026-11-20); the shops' walk stops short of it. Hostels (owner's sketches and satellite shots, 2026-11-20, reference/satellite/boys_hostels_track_chenab.jpg): the boys' side south of the track is four separate L hostels (Yamuna, Sindhu, Boys Hostel 3 and 4) staggered round a lawn open east to `Hostel East Road`, and the girls' side is four L hostels (A-D: B and C a U round the north courtyard with the lane along their front, A and D stacked west of the other courtyard); every L is the same size (owner: both arms equally long and thick: two 14 m deep wings, each arm 32 m from the outer corner); each L has its entrances at the inside corner where its wings meet (`INNER_CORNER_HOSTELS`, `hostel_wing_doors` in build_buildings.py, replayed by `facade_sides` in build_hostel_interiors.py). There is no fountain at the top of the clock tower walk (owner); the walk's steps run straight up to the porch drive's edge, with white planter boxes stepping down both sides, and the academic front has a two-storey colonnade of square pillars along both side blocks and rows of columns under the porch (owner's photos in project files reference/photos/academic_front/); `Southern Road East` runs round the track's south end and up its east side and ends at the Chenab roundabout by Chenab Hostel; `Track Road` only reaches the East Block.
- Terrain detail pass (2026-11-20, from the owner's two videos: a Unity terrain tutorial and a realistic-graphics guide): generate_terrain.py also writes `terrain_details.bytes` (five density layers: lawn grass, wild grass, yellow flowers, white flowers, grass clumps; level ground inside the wall is mown, long grass on banks, the hill and outside) and paints ragged worn verges and dry lawn edges. `CampusTerrainBuilder.DetailPrototypes` turns them into billboard cards (`GrassTexture`, `FlowerTexture`) and a mesh clump (`GrassClumpPrefab`, Grass mode, waves by vertex alpha); counts are the High tier's (raised 2026-11-21 when the owner asked to be generous with grass: lawn 20, wild 14, flowers 5 each, clumps 6 per cell, 20-blade clumps, drawn to 120 m), lower tiers thin them through `detailObjectDensity` (`useDensityScaling`). Each terrain layer has a channel-packed mask map (`MaskFor`: AO, height, smoothness) and the terrain material uses height blending (4 layers max, keep it at 4). Tree prefabs have two LODs and a cull size (`SaveTree`). High is the default quality (owner: quality over frame rate). `tools/blender/preview_terrain_detail.py` mirrors the textures, sizes and densities for before/after mock-ups, so change them together.
- Terrain: `tools/layout/generate_terrain.py` (numpy) writes the heightmap, splat, grass and tree data; `--preview` draws a top-down check image into `tools/layout/preview/` (gitignored). Its foothill-ridge shape is a design, not survey data; the owner asked for strong relief (flat terrain was rejected twice). East of Hostel East Road the ground rises as a steep wooded hill almost as tall as the hostels (owner, 2026-11-20; `SCARPS` in generate_terrain.py: a foot line, level ground before it, a steep face and a lumpy top). East of the track and the girls' hostels the ground is not a hill but a drop (owner, 2026-11-21): the campus stays level to a stone retaining wall and then falls steeply to low ground (`DROPS` and `DROP_*` in generate_terrain.py, re-applied after the blur so the step stays sharp; the wall line goes to campus_terrain.json as `retainingWalls`, built by `retaining_wall()` in build_outdoor.py as `Retaining_Wall_<n>`: rubble face battered down to the foot, piers, weep pipes, coping walk and parapet; OutdoorBuilder gives it a MeshCollider and a long cull distance). The mess area sits on a terrace about 10-12 m above the girls' hostels and the faculty residences a little higher (`TERRACE_PADS`, owner's instruction). Per the owner's satellite shots (2026-11-19) the spine road is the only road to Annapurna Mess and runs level past the mess and shop fronts (`MESS_LEVEL`, `MESS_ROAD_LEVEL_Z`); the drop is a bank with railings on the road's east side, and there is no retaining wall (the owner rejected it). `tools/blender/render_terrain_views.py [view names]` renders eye-level checks of the terrain into `tools/blender/preview/`. Road heights are solved as one network (`solve_road_profiles`): wider roads first, a road that meets or crosses a solved road or roundabout takes its height there, and each profile is held under `ROAD_GRADE` (9-10 % drives); the ground is laid to them after the plots and terraces, and the plots are levelled again without touching road surfaces, so add new shaping before the road pass. The serpentine's heights are set along its length (pinned `CLOCK_STEPS_RISE` below the porch where the clock tower walk meets it) and the hillside between its legs is shaped from it. The clock tower walk is not a road: `CLOCK_STEPS` in generate_layout.py, flights and landings designed in generate_terrain.py and written to campus_terrain.json (`clockSteps`), built by `clock_steps()` in build_buildings.py, so run build_buildings.py after the terrain. The amphitheatre plot sits `AMPHITHEATRE_DEPTH` below the academic plot (top walk level with the ring road). Trees: the owner's photos show few trees on the campus (2026-11-19), so there are no road avenues except the gate approach, only a sprinkle of bushes and neem inside the campus, chir pines only on the hill by the courts, and scrub forest outside; the pine is a tall trunk with a flat, open crown, never stacked cones. Vegetation (2026-11-21, owner: the old trees and bushes were ugly cubes): `Assets/Editor/Vegetation.cs` builds every tree kind (0 broadleaf, 1 eucalyptus, 2 chir pine, 3 shrub, 4 flowering shrub, 5 tall grass; generate_terrain.py writes kinds 4 and 5) from branches and alpha-cut leaf cards with painted leaf atlases (lit and shaded rows, coverage-preserving mips) on `Jagti/Foliage` (`_Cutoff`, `_BackFlip`); props carry leaf-card slots (`Leaf_Broad`, `Leaf_Shrub`, `Leaf_Red`, `Leaf_Flower`, `Palm_Frond`, `Leaf_Hedge`, `Leaf_Grass`, given by `Vegetation.LeafMaterial` in BuildingMaterials). `tools/blender/plants.py` mirrors it in Python (render_map_tour's trees and leaf materials, `prop_cards`/`bush_cards`/`hedge_cards` for the Blender props, `preview_vegetation.py`), so change the two together. Never draw foliage as a solid box or blob; a hedge's core box stays small and hidden inside its cards.
- C# type check without Unity: `apt-get install mono-mcs`, take UnityEngine.dll/UnityEditor.dll from the NuGet package `unity3d.sdk` 2021.1.14.1, stub URP and Unity.AI.Navigation types, and compile with `mcs`. This catches typos but not Unity 6-only API differences.
- The scene is generated by `Assets/Editor/CampusGreyboxBuilder.cs` (menu Jagti > Build Campus); do not hand-edit `Campus_Greybox.unity`. Textures and tree models are made from code into `Assets/Generated` (gitignored).
- Input: classic Input Manager (`UnityEngine.Input`), not the Input System package.
- Performance targets: 30 fps on Iris Xe, 0.8 render scale, baked lighting, fog-limited draw distance, zone streaming later.
- Blender works headless in the container through `pip install bpy` (Python 3.13; `import bpy` before `bmesh`). `tools/blender/build_buildings.py` builds the landmark FBX files from campus_layout.json (`--render` writes Cycles previews to `tools/blender/preview/`, gitignored). Git LFS uploads are blocked from the cloud container (lfs.github.com is denied), so the small generated FBX files are plain Git (see .gitattributes); keep them small. Material slot names must match `Assets/Editor/BuildingMaterials.cs`, and the tile sizes in both files must agree. Unity checks each model's bounds against building_models.json and corrects scale, turn or mirror. Coplanar faces flicker black in Unity, so `Model.to_object` runs `separate_coplanar`, which lifts one face of each same-facing overlapping pair by a few millimetres; interior shells are also lifted off their building's faces (`exterior_faces` in build_interiors.py). Keep that pass, and don't count on exact coincidence between faces. Buildings from different layout entries must not touch, because each builds its full facade (Girls Hostel A and B are 4 m apart). Roof access: `FIRE_ESCAPES` in build_buildings.py puts a steel switchback stair (`fire_escape`) against one door-free outer wall of each big building, up to the parapet coping, with a plate over it onto the roof. Sites were checked for level ground, roads, doors and other buildings, so recheck them when a building moves. The running track and its infield are `pill` areas: straights joined by half circles. Every area consumer handles `pill` (generate_terrain `pad_distance`, build_outdoor `in_area`, render_map_tour, `MakePillArea` in CampusGreyboxBuilder, and the RoyaleBuilder minimap).
- Building names come from the official campus plan. Treat names marked "?" on the vision map as guesses.
- Gameplay (Phase 3) lives in `Assets/Scripts/Gameplay/` (namespace Jagti.Gameplay); `Assets/Editor/GameplayBuilder.cs` wires it into the scene after the NavMesh bake. Zombies, view models and sounds are built in code at runtime (no prefabs or audio files yet). Each MonoBehaviour needs its own file named after the class.
- Interiors (Phase 4): `tools/blender/build_interiors.py` (run after `build_buildings.py`) writes the interior shell FBX, furniture FBX files in `Assets/Models/Props/` and `Assets/Data/interiors.json`; `Assets/Editor/InteriorBuilder.cs` places them before the NavMesh bake. Exterior doorways are cut in `build_buildings.py` (OUTER_DOORS, courtyard middle bays, BAY_DOOR_SEGMENTS, EAST_DOOR_Z); the interior walls copy the facade's window positions through `facade_openings`, so keep the two in step. Rooms are few and big (owner, 2026-11-21: the buildings had far too many rooms): hostel bands join plain bays into rooms up to `ROOM_MAX` (11 m) wide (`big_rooms` in build_hostel_interiors.py; big rooms are dorms, common and study rooms), core rooms are about 11 m, and the academic block has open faculty offices (`f_faculty`) instead of cabins and two syndicate rooms a side. Room doorways are at least 1.1 m wide (the player capsule caught on 0.9 m ones). `clear_doorways` (build_interiors.py) finds every door threshold in the building models and drops ground-floor furniture that would block it; a hostel entrance keeps its lobby even when it falls in a corner room. Interior floors sit 0.6 m above each storey line. New material slots go in MATERIALS and `InteriorMaterials.cs`. Upper storeys: `level_openings` mirrors `academic_facade` floor by floor, and `hall_bays` (build_buildings) leaves the west facade open where the Mandapam hall covers it; change both sides together. Each level starts from the ground plan (`make_level`); furniture and lights carry a `level`, and `InteriorZone` draws only the camera's storey and its neighbours. Hostels and the mess (Phase 4C) are in `tools/blender/build_hostel_interiors.py`, imported by `build_interiors.py` (one shell and zone per building; props and lights carry `zone`, and zones carry `floorOffset`/`maxLevel`). Hostel rooms follow `hostel_facade` bay by bay (`facade_sides` replays it, and both take each window from `hostel_glass` and `HOSTEL_WIN_Z`), so change the two together; the hostel look (paired deep-set windows, jali stair cores between fins, solar roofs) is from the owner's photos and the satellite view, see project memory; hostel wings in the layout must touch, not overlap, so their corridors can be joined. The mess and medical interiors are drawn in world coordinates at their first spot (`INTERIOR_DESIGN` in build_buildings.py) and shifted to the layout's position, so the layout may move those buildings but must keep their sizes. Shops, the medical centre and the sports complex (Phase 4D) are in `tools/blender/build_civic_interiors.py` (plans written room by room in world coordinates); their shop fronts and doorways come from `SHOP_UNITS`, `SHOP_SIDE` and `CIVIC_DOORS` in `build_buildings.py`, which both files read. The shops are Vipanan (owner's video and photo, 2026-11-19): a single-storey L of two arms (`vipanan()` in build_buildings.py: raised colonnade, deep white fascia band, roof shelter and solar panels) round a paved `plaza` area; its units are furnished from their own shop fronts (`UnitFrame`), so the arms may change size.
- Outdoor props (Phase 4D): `tools/blender/build_outdoor.py` (run after the terrain and `build_buildings.py`) writes the street furniture FBX files and `Assets/Data/outdoor_props.json`; `Assets/Editor/OutdoorBuilder.cs` places them and sets sign text with TextMesh. Placement follows the layout (roads, paths, lawns, courts, places) and the terrain heightmap, so rerun it after layout or terrain changes. Street details from the owner's ground photos (2026-11-19): feather flags along every drive, both sides of the spine, porch drive and gate approach (`street_flags`), a shrub-bordered lawn island between Vipanan and the mess (`mess_island`), the kerbed bed, young tree and map board at the split to the girls' hostels (`girls_split`, where `Paved Girls Hostel Lane` leaves the spine road), and rubble stone walls with floodlight masts on the courts' west and north sides where the bank rises (`court_walls`). Low shrubs line the verges of roads 4.5 m or wider inside the campus (the verge block in generate_terrain.py).
- Battle royale (Phase 5, the default mode): runtime code in `Assets/Scripts/Royale/` (namespace Jagti.Royale); `Assets/Editor/RoyaleBuilder.cs` wires it in after the NavMesh bake. **Jagti > Game Mode** (EditorPrefs `Jagti.GameMode`) picks royale or zombie survival for the next build. Loot spots come from `tools/royale/build_loot_points.py` → `Assets/Data/loot_points.json` (run after `build_interiors.py`; `--preview` draws `tools/royale/preview/`). It keeps loot off furniture using `tools/royale/prop_sizes.json`, which `tools/royale/measure_props.py` (bpy) rebuilds when props change. Gun, item and brand names are original; copy no names, characters, art, UI or items from existing battle royale games. Bots (5B): `HumanBody.cs` builds a skinned body with `MeshKit.cs`; `tools/royale/preview_bodies.py` is a numpy port of its build and poses for Blender previews, so change the two together. Difficulty, the think scheduler and the cover search are in `BotTuning.cs`. Guns and attachments (5C): models in `LootVisuals.cs` use one gun frame (barrel +X, top +Z, thickness Y around Yc), with attachment mount points in `MountsFor`; `tools/royale/preview_guns.py` parses that C# to render them, so keep the call forms simple (P, Barrel, b.Box, b.Tube, b.Ball, and one-line `for (int i = 0; i < N; i++) P(...)` loops); `--hero` renders a close-up. The view model's sway, sprint, landing, reload and recoil motion is in `PlayerArsenal.AnimateView`, and the muzzle flash (light plus crossed quads) sits at each gun's muzzle mount. Getting around (5D, 6E): `Vehicle.cs` (four kinds: scooter, utility cart, auto-rickshaw, patrol 4x4; driving, Shift boost tank, H horn, damage) with models in `VehicleModels.cs` (the loot palette, which now has Yellow and Green), `VehicleSpawner.cs` (parking spots found by RoyaleBuilder along roads and car parks), `ZipLine.cs` (sites found by RoyaleBuilder; `tools/royale/preview_getting_around.py` repeats the search on the heightmap) and `PlayerMover.cs` (vaulting, wading in the channels, riding, zipping). `tools/royale/preview_vehicles.py [--fleet]` translates the VehicleModels/ZipLine model code to Python to render it, so keep those to MeshKit calls, simple declarations, foreach over literal arrays and plain for loops. Map for battle (5E): cover models and placement come from `tools/royale/build_cover.py` (bpy; garden cover only since 2026-11-21: hedge beds, bush clumps and low garden walls, because the owner wanted the pipes, bricks, drums and barriers "laying around" gone; run after `build_outdoor.py` and `build_loot_points.py`) → `Assets/Models/Props/Cover_*.fbx` and `Assets/Data/cover_props.json` (outdoor_props format, placed by OutdoorBuilder in royale builds before the NavMesh bake); rerun it after layout, terrain or outdoor prop changes. `PropCuller` (Assets/Scripts/World) hides outdoor props past per-prop distances set in OutdoorBuilder. Online (5F): `Assets/Scripts/Net` (namespace Jagti.Net, plain C# with no Unity types: `NetPeer` UDP transport, `NetBuffer`) is tested by `tools/net/net_test.cs` with mono (see its header); `Assets/Scripts/Royale/Online` has `NetSession` (DontDestroyOnLoad connection and lobby), `NetLobbyMenu` (front menu; MatchDirector waits for it), `NetMatch` (in-match messages, types in `NetMsg`) and `NetPuppet`. Rules to keep: every machine runs the match from the host's seed, so `LootDirector.SpawnAll`, `PlanFlight` (the first use of MatchDirector's rng), haze centres and `SupplyDrop.Land` must stay deterministic; `Combatant.owned` decides who applies a hit (host owns bots, each player owns themselves; `ReceiveHit` forwards hits on others), so new damage goes through `ReceiveHit`; new loot changes go through `LootDirector.Add/Remove/SetCount`; new throws through `Grenade.Launch`; shots through `Ballistics.Fire`. Online the pause menu doesn't stop time (check `Time.timeScale == 0f`, not just `GameState.Paused`, before freezing match logic).
- HUD and menus (Phase 6A): IMGUI drawn through `Assets/Scripts/Royale/Ui/HudKit.cs` in reference units of a 1080-high screen (palette, generated textures, widgets; draws only on repaint, `useGUILayout = false`). `RoyaleHud` is a partial class: `RoyaleHud.cs` (state, compass, kill feed), `.Combat.cs` (player card, hotbar, ammo, crosshair, drop), `.Maps.cs` (minimap, haze timer, full map), `.Menus.cs` (pause, results); `NetLobbyMenu` is the front menu and shares `MenuPanels` (settings, controls). Settings live in `GameSettings` (PlayerPrefs). Icons are shape strings in `HudIcons.cs`; fonts are Barlow Condensed (OFL) in `Assets/Resources/Fonts` (plain Git, see .gitattributes). `tools/royale/preview_hud.py BACKGROUND.png [MAP.png]` mirrors the layouts with Pillow for mockups, so change the two together.
- Sky bus (Phase 6B): the drop craft is `SkyBus.cs` (flight, burner, sounds) with its model in `SkyBusModel.cs` (campus bus under a hot-air balloon, own 9-slot palette made in RoyaleBuilder). `tools/royale/preview_skybus.py` renders it through the preview_vehicles.py translator, so keep the model code in the same simple forms.
- Loot stashes and rich pickings (Phase 6C): `build_loot_points.py` also writes `stashes` (trunk spots against room walls from `STASH_ROOMS`, a few outside buildings; own rng stream so loot points don't move). `LootDirector.SpawnAll` picks two rich-pickings places (fuller, better floor loot), then fills stashes with sealed items (`LootStash`, `LootDrop.Sealed`) that open with E (`NetMsg.Stash` online). The trunk model is `StashModel.cs`, rendered by `tools/royale/preview_stash.py` through the preview_vehicles.py translator.
- Player feel and glass (2026-11-19): the player capsule is 1.75 m tall, 0.28 m radius (the old 0.35 m jammed in 1 m doorways), eye at 1.58 m, default field of view 70; `FirstPersonController` sets that capsule in Awake (old scenes kept a bigger one), eases speed (ground/air acceleration), allows a coyote jump and a buffered jump press, snaps to the ground going down slopes and stairs, smooths the eye over steps and ducks by itself under low openings (`AutoDuck`). Keep 2 m of headroom over every walkable floor and doorway (the case-room tiers rise 0.12 m a row under the 2.5 m ceilings; shop-door shutter boxes are slim). Riding: the chase view trails the vehicle's heading (`rideTurnLag`) and pivots on the level seat with bumps smoothed (PlayerMover.LateUpdate). Room lights fade in and out (`InteriorZone`). Windows are breakable: `GlassSplitter` (editor, run from `BuildingModels.Place`, and from `InteriorBuilder` for the shells' Glass_Clear) moves each building's glass submesh into a child mesh cut into panes (big glass walls into ~1.6 m panes; meshes saved to Assets/Generated/Glass) and the building keeps the rest as its collider; `GlassPanes` (Assets/Scripts/World) has one thin BoxCollider per pane, saved with the scene under "Breakable Glass" (so the NavMesh bake sees windows as walls). Every facade window also has the interior shell's clear pane behind it; breaking either breaks its twin (`BreakTwins`, on every machine), so never leave an unbreakable pane in a window. Shots pass through and break glass (`Ballistics.Trace`), sprinting, jumping or falling into it breaks it (`PlayerMover.OnControllerColliderHit`), vehicles and grenades too; local breaks go online as `NetMsg.Glass`. Bodies use `MeshKit.Loft` (smooth elliptical rings) for torso, limbs, head, shoes, vest and pack; mirror any change in `preview_bodies.py` (`--hero` renders a half-size close-up).
- Look pass (Phase 6 polish): `Assets/Editor/AtmosphereBuilder.cs` sets the sun, sky, trilight ambient and exponential fog, makes the global post-processing volume (ACES, grading, split toning, bloom, vignette), adds SSAO to the URP renderer (through serialized fields, since its settings class is internal), a realtime reflection probe, wind, ambient dust and leaves, and the impact materials; bump `LookVersion` there so JagtiProjectSetup rebuilds the scene. Runtime: `VisualQuality` (Low/Medium/High in the settings menu: render scale, shadows, SSAO, bloom, AA, grass), `WindDriver` (global `_JagtiWind` for `Assets/Shaders/JagtiFoliage.shader`, which the tree materials use when it compiles), `AmbientParticles`, `ImpactMarks` (sparks, dirt and bullet marks, called from Ballistics). `tools/blender/preview_look.py` mocks up before/after by shading Cycles light passes the Unity way and applying the grade in numpy; keep its numbers in step with AtmosphereBuilder. Look pass 2 (2026-11-20, from the owner's porch photos in project files reference/photos/sky/: the sunset shot for the day sky, the dusk shot for night): `Assets/Shaders/JagtiSky.shader` (painted gradient sky, cirrus clouds, stars, far ridges; procedural sky if it fails to compile), `DayCycle` (Assets/Scripts/World; Time of day in the settings menu: Day, Sunset, Night, Changing = sunset into night over `CycleMinutes`; one `Look` per mood drives sun, trilight, fog, sky material, exposure and lamps) and `StreetLights` (lamp heads listed by OutdoorBuilder from each lamp prop's glow submesh; at night the glow material brightens, every lamp gets a halo and a ground pool drawn by two fixed particle systems, and the nearest 4/8/14 lamps by quality tier get real lights). `tools/blender/preview_sky.py` ports the sky shader and the Looks to numpy for mock-ups, so change the three together. Feather flags (`Feather_Flag_<Colour>`, nine colours, `street_flags` in build_outdoor.py) have one sail slot `Flag_Cloth` that OutdoorBuilder swaps for a waving cloth material (Jagti/Foliage with `_Wave` and `_Cull` off; the wave reads uv.x 0 at the pole to 1 at the free edge, uv.y in metres). Surfaces: roads are pale concrete slabs (`AsphaltTexture` kept its name), kerbs black and white, and `ProceduralAssets.AddRelief` (normal maps from each texture's own brightness) and `AddMacroVariation` (URP detail albedo at low tiling, linear grey 0.5 = no change) go on roads, walls, facades and terrain layers.
- Helicopter and lifts (6F): `Vehicle.Kind.Heli` flies with arcade controls (W/S, A/D, Space up, Ctrl/C down, Shift boost; `Fly`, sphere-cast collisions, `SurfaceBelow` landing), model `Heli` in VehicleModels.cs (`preview_vehicles.py --heli`). `Helipad.cs` is built in code; RoyaleBuilder's `PlaceHelipad` puts it on the academic block's roof and VehicleSpawner parks the helicopter there. `Lift.cs` rides from a ground door to a roof hut (or a raised deck over solar panels): the camera pulls out, the building turns into an additive ghost and a storey-by-storey skeleton frame lights the floor the car passes (Space/E skips). `PlaceLifts` in RoyaleBuilder finds the spots (existing `Lift_Door` props first, entrance lobbies otherwise); `tools/royale/preview_lifts.py` repeats that search in Blender (`--check` writes preview/lift_spots.json) and renders `--heli` and `--cutaway ZONE`, so change the two together. The Medical Centre has no lift (no free wall found).
- Polish and quality of life (2026-11-20): `SpectatorCam` (Assets/Scripts/Royale; runs last, `DefaultExecutionOrder(1000)`) takes over Camera.main when the player dies: it unparents the camera from the head, plays the death camera (rise out of the head, look at the body, turn to the killer, brief slow motion offline only) and, from the results screen's SPECTATE button, follows living fighters over the shoulder (LMB/RMB cycle, mouse orbits, Enter/Esc back to results). It only moves the local camera, so nothing goes online; bot and body LODs follow Camera.main, so the watched fighter animates fully. `LoadingScreen` (Royale/Ui) replaces the frozen reload: `MatchDirector.Reload` fades out, loads with LoadSceneAsync offline (a plain LoadScene online so messages never reach a half-loaded scene) and fades in; it clears `Combatant.All` just before the load. Settings are two pages (`MenuPanels.SettingsPage`: GAME, SCREEN AND SOUND; `SettingsPanelHeight`) with RESET ALL; new ones are invert look, aim sensitivity (the look turn is scaled by the zoom, tan ratio of the fields of view, in `PlayerArsenal.SetAim` through `FirstPersonController.lookScale`), toggle aim, toggle sprint and pause-and-mute in the background (`RoyaleHud.OnApplicationFocus`). T or middle mouse drops the marker where the crosshair points (again on it clears it). The haze ticks for the last five seconds before it moves. `preview_hud.py` mirrors the death camera, spectate bar and both settings pages.
